    Why can’t I connect to my realm?

    When a realm server is taken down or goes down during a game of Diablo II: Lord of Destruction, it can no longer send data to the client for areas too far off the screen. Because no data is being sent to the client, the map will not be drawn beyond that point. Should this happen, we suggest you Save and Exit and then reconnect to Battle.net.
