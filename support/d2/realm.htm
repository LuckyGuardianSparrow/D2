    Why can’t I connect to my Realm?

    If you cannot create a character on your desired Realm or cannot log onto the Realm with an existing character, please download the Gateway Selector from our FTP site to select manually which gateway you connect to when you log onto Battle.net.

    PC: Download

    MAC: Download

    After running the Gateway Selector and choosing the Realm on which you wish to play, run Diablo II: Lord of Destruction, connect to Battle.net, and log in with your Realm character.
