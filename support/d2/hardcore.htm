    What is a Hardcore game?

      A Hardcore game is identical to a standard game, except that if your character dies, it cannot be resurrected. Once it dies, it's dead forever, and it cannot join or create games. Dead Hardcore characters are depicted as Grey-robed Figures in Battle.net chat channels.


    How do I play Hardcore games?

      First, you will need to create a Hardcore character. To do this, you will need to complete Diablo II: Lord of Destruction with one of your normal characters. After you have completed Diablo II: Lord of Destruction, the next time you create a character, there will be a check box labeled "Hardcore." Check this to create a Hardcore character. Hardcore characters can only create or join Hardcore games.
