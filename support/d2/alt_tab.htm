    Diablo II: Lord of Destruction Keeps Exiting To Windows:

      Some programs can cause Diablo II: Lord of Destruction to minimize, or may bleed through the game screen. To correct this, close all other applications before starting Diablo II: Lord of Destruction.


    Steps to close programs running in the background:
      Press the <Ctrl>, <Alt>, and <Delete> keys at the same time to bring up the Windows Task Manager. End task on all programs except for Explorer and systray.
      Note: These programs will restart when you reboot your computer.


    Steps to modify your startup files:

    • Click start, run, then type "msconfig" and push enter.
    • Select the Startup tab
    • Uncheck all the boxes and click OK.
    • When the "System Settings Change" window appears, click ok to restart the computer.
      Note: To start these prgrams again all you need to do is run msconfig, recheck the boxes, and reboot your computer.
