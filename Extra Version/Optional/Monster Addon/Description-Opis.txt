English
-------
Adds 17 new monster variants that replace normal monsters in the unused locations.

Decaying Chambers - Stinger
Shaded Cavity - Stinger

Sandstone Nest - Lavender Beetle, Shriekbat
Vile Crypt Level 1 - Blight Beak
Lost Tomb - Soul Husk
Swarmed Abyss - Dreadwing, Phantom Biters
Desolate Sands - Dust Lord, Dusk Clan
Abandoned Floor - Magog, Dreadwing Nest

Overgrown Stronghold - Forest Panther
Stronghold Basement - Forest Slinger
Warped Temple - Punished

Agony Dungeon - Abyss Spawn
Agony Depths - Magma Swimmer
Dread Ruins - Bone Gulper, Mana Seeker
Altar of Blood - Negator

Note: This mod is incompatible with Evil Wolves Enabled,  Magic Resist Addon and Original Baal's Champions
